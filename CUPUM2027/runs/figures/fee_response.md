One row per arm and peak charge (peak_fee, NZ$: the ToU schedule scaled to that peak, charged from the charge day) over days 6-10. Peak 0 is the no-charge run of the same arm and seed (run.py --peak-fee 0). car_crossings_change_pct, the main measure, compares the car cordon crossings per day with that run on the same days, so only the charge differs; car_crossings_change_vs_own_d2_5_pct is the run's own change against its uncharged days 2-5 (the before/after measure the behaviour calibration targets), which also holds what changes over the days without a charge. same_before_charge_as_peak0 checks that decisions.csv and outcomes.csv of the days before the charge equal the peak-0 run's, as they must (same seed, no charge yet). travel_share = car + PT, the share of commuters going into the city centre.

Willingness to travel: wtt_limited_share and wtt_no_car_share are the shares of commuter-days with at least one and with every car option removed because its charge was above the commuter's willingness to pay; wtp_below_peak_share is the share of commuters whose willingness to pay is below the peak charge (company cars and commuters with neither PT nor WFH keep their car options all the same). Delays in minutes: mean_road_delay of car commuters; peak_queue_delay the peak 15-min gate-queue delay; peak_total_delay the peak 15-min gate-queue + road delay, the metric the capacity is calibrated to (15 min without a charge). LoS over 07:00-09:00, mwy = motorways, other = other roads: los_ef_speed_share = share of traffic at LoS E/F by speed, los_ef_share = at LoS E/F by V/C, vc_mean = the mean V/C, congested_at_share = congested by AT's definition. LoS E/F by V/C and the mean V/C are shown together and are best read together. On other roads the V/C grade is E/F for about 99% of the traffic from 07:00, because their capacity is a BPR scale fitted to the TomTom congestion index (about 0.077 x the daily traffic count), not a lane capacity. On motorways about 76% of the vehicle-km is inside the fixed usual breakdown windows, which are graded F whatever the flow. LoS E/F by speed (at most 40% of free-flow speed) and the AT congested share (below 50%) grade the simulated speeds.

Behaviour is calibrated at the NZ$6 schedule; other peaks extrapolate. The L arms use MockLLM (mock), a pipeline check, not an LLM.
