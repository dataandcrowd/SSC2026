LoS E/F by V/C and the mean V/C are shown together and are best read together. On other roads the V/C grade is E/F for about 99% of the traffic from 07:00, because their capacity is a BPR scale fitted to the TomTom congestion index (about 0.077 x the daily traffic count), not a lane capacity. On motorways about 76% of the vehicle-km is inside the fixed usual breakdown windows, which are graded F whatever the flow. LoS E/F by speed (at most 40% of free-flow speed) and the AT congested share (below 50%) grade the simulated speeds.
